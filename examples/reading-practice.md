# 原创练习 · A Library After Dark

本篇为原创练习。读完后提交 `46A 47B` 这样的答案格式，并在拿不准的选项后加 `?`。

When a city library extended its opening hours, staff expected the extra time to attract mostly university students. Instead, the evening visitors included shift workers, parents who could only leave home after their children went to bed, and residents who needed a quiet place to complete job applications. Because the library had previously closed at six, few of these visitors had appeared in its surveys. Those surveys had been handed out at the building's entrance during opening hours.

The city initially judged the trial by counting visits. But the library also asked evening visitors what they would have done if the building had been closed. Some said they would have worked at home. Others explained that they would have postponed an application or gone without internet access. This distinction mattered because ten people arriving at the library might represent ten extra visits while only a few had gained access to something they otherwise lacked. The staff argued that counting visits was useful, but it could not tell the whole story.

A. They did not consider libraries useful.  
B. The surveys were conducted when many of them could not visit.  
C. The library only gave surveys to university students.  
D. They preferred to complete surveys online.

**47. What does the passage suggest about using visit counts to evaluate the trial?**

A. Visit counts tell staff how often users postpone applications.  
B. Visit counts offer little information about demand for evening hours.  
C. Visit counts alone cannot show which visitors gained access they otherwise lacked.  
D. Visit counts rise only when visitors have no alternatives.
